My Pets

Here I will share information about my two dogs Cooper and Cali.

Cooper

Cooper is my dog. He is a very lazy and old but cute black lab.

We have had him for around 7 years and he is a beloved member of our family even if he does refuse to listen sometimes.

He enjoys long naps and baking in the sun. He is a dog who is not very active and prefers a calm environment. He will take any chance to get any kind of attention or treat he can get.

Cali

Cali is my second dog. She is a very energetic and playful chocolate lab-husky mix.

We have had her for around 4 years and she is also a beloved member of our family, even if she thinks the house is hers and she can do whatever she wants too.

She loves to play with fetch and eat bones. Even though she is very weary of men, she is very much willing to make new friends if she gets to know you enough.

She, like Cooper, loves attention and treats, and will even playfully fight Cooper for it sometimes.

C&C Dynamic Duo

She alongside Cooper make a lively duo in our household, they both keep us entertained with their goofiness and cuteness.

They are truly the heart of our home and bring immense joy to the house. Without them, it would be a much quieter and sadder place, and I would not have something to always make me happy (even if they do irritate me sometimes) every day.
